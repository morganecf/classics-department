Bet on Love

A surreal musical odyssey of romance and live betting

Dating. Prediction markets. Third-act arias straight from Les Misérables. A mysterious train drawing ever closer.

What happens when a Bay Area startup hires a team of irreverent artists to create lore that will go down in history?

You get Bet on Love, a surreal musical odyssey of romance and live betting! Guided by an existentially troubled CEO (Duncan Horst) and his hapless Blood Boy (Tom Low), six contestants compete in a series of challenges for the chance to win a date with Aella, our glamorous bachelorette. As markets track their performance, contestants must flirt, dance, and neg, fend off a bloodthirsty polycule, consummate their marriage under the watch of a mysterious nurse, and most importantly, come to terms with Aella’s death before the Singularity hits.
