Psychomagic 9

An experiment in playable theater

The sold-out immersive show inspired by the 1973 cult classic The Holy Mountain

Get ticket alerts 2025 program

“Truly operating at the cutting edge of immersive theatre… This is the realest, most underground interactive performance art show on the scene right now.”

D.S.

“The skill of the spectator determines the machine’s ability to reach a climax.”

Alejandro Jodorowsky

On a legendary production poised on the brink of glory, a film crew’s patience wears dangerously thin. Presiding over them is the Alchemist, a poetic wonderworker and disaster provocateur whose delusions of grandeur have brought production to a standstill.

As the Alchemist draws the set deeper into a world of magic and madness, guests find themselves entangled in increasingly intricate quests to get the production back on track. Their goal: to guide the nine lost souls of the cast & crew towards a personal miracle before the end of the night…

Psychomagic 9 is an Equity approved Showcase.

Every show is unique

A sly, intoxicating work of theater and choose-your-own-adventure storytelling

You’ll play a direct part in the unfolding of the story, interacting with a cast of chaotic improvisers in a game that evokes the cabin-fever excitement of a film set, with all its drama and disaster.

How quickly you act, and the choices you make, will impact the outcome of the story. So tread wisely, and choose your path with style. Psychomagic 9 is designed for the bold.

90 players. 4 possible endings. 9 main characters caught in the thrall of a mysterious traveling alchemist.
