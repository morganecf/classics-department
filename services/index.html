Event production services

People are tired of traditional media experiences.

Most interactive events miss the mark by watering down their content for mass consumption. Afraid of taking risks, they leave people feeling annoyed, bored, and a little more alienated than before.

But humans crave fresh, exciting encounters that resonate with meaning. They naturally seek out peak experiences, delicious surprises, and ways to bridge the gap between their hearts and others’.

Why not give them that?

We work tirelessly, with reckless ambition, to create custom productions that speak directly to your audience and build community. Our events have been called “life-changing” and “better than Sleep No More.”

We’re based in New York City, but for the right project, we’ll travel anywhere in the U.S. Just reach out to classicsdept@proton.me, and let’s talk.

Tools of our trade

How we work

Writing & Conception

We work closely with you to understand your goals, then write a script or an outline to get your vision on paper. For narrative productions, we’re interested in larger-than-life characters, daring storylines that speak to contemporary issues, and worlds layered with mystery, absurdism, and heart.

Artistic Design

We collaborate with you to develop a distinctive aesthetic to guide all artistic aspects of production, including sound design, lighting, music composition, costume creation, and room decoration — whether that’s on a stage or on location. We love transforming ordinary spaces into magical environments.

Production

We orchestrate all the behind-the-scenes details of a production: finding a venue, hiring collaborators, sourcing equipment, and coordinating the various logistics it takes to bring your dream to life.

Performance

Theater, performance art, ritual, music! We have experience with a wide range of performances, and we leverage an extensive network of talented collaborators, from songwriters and musicians to skilled actors, for each new piece.

Filmmaking

If you want to add cinematic flair to live shows, expand fictional worlds into multi-media universes, or simply generate some marketing content, we can produce short films and short-form video content on a scrappy budget.

Game Design

Gamification is a surefire way to kick your event into overdrive! We design alternative reality games, live-action roleplaying games, murder mysteries, and authentic relating events.
