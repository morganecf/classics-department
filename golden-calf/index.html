Welcome to the Herd of God

A church for holy rollers, gnostic knowers, and millenarians of the coming dawn

We worship the one god who’s been there all along: the sacred bull, the humble heifer, the one and only holy cow.

Join us to advance the plot and remember what the rest forgot!
