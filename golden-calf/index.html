Welcome to the Herd of God

A church for holy rollers, gnostic knowers, and millenarians of the coming dawn!

Reality is changing fast. Nothing is quite as it seems.

We worship the one god who’s been there all along: the sacred bull, the humble heifer, the one and only holy cow.

We use all the newest technologies to prepare you for the glories and devastation of the Aquarian Age. We are well-capitalized and we are the aggregate sum of the brightest people on planet earth, including YOU, who still remain hungry for divine truth.

Join us to advance the plot and remember what the rest forgot!
